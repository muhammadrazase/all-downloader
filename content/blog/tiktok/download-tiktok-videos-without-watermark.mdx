Wanting to download TikTok videos without a watermark is one of the most common creator and viewer requests, and for good reason. The default in-app save stamps your clip with a moving watermark and the username, which looks messy when you repurpose the video elsewhere or edit it into something new. This guide walks through how to get a clean copy on mobile and PC, without installing sketchy apps.

## Why the in-app download is not enough

TikTok's built-in save feature exists mainly for sharing to other social apps, not for professional reuse. The watermark it adds bounces around the frame and burns the username into the footage. That is fine for a casual repost, but it is a problem if you want to edit the clip, feature it in a compilation, or run it through your own branding.

A watermark-free copy gives you a clean canvas. You keep full resolution, you lose the distracting overlay, and you can build on the footage properly.

## How to download on iPhone

iOS is slightly stricter about where files go, so the destination matters. After you paste the link and start the download:

- Safari will prompt you to save the file. Choose Downloads or Files.
- To move it into your camera roll, open the Files app, find the video, tap Share, and select Save Video.

If you are using an in-app browser, such as the one inside another social app, switch to Safari first. In-app browsers sometimes block downloads or hide the save prompt.

## How to download on Android

Android is more flexible with file handling. After pasting the link and starting the download:

- Chrome saves the file to your Downloads folder automatically.
- Open your Gallery or Files app to find it, and it will usually appear in your video library without extra steps.

If your gallery does not show it immediately, give the media scanner a moment or reopen the gallery app to refresh.
