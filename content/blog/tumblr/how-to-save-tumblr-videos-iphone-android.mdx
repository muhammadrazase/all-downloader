You found a great clip on Tumblr, tapped and held on it, and got nothing useful. That's the usual experience on mobile, because Tumblr's app doesn't include a save button. The good news is that saving a video to your phone is quick once you know the two-tap trick, and it works the same on both iOS and Android.

This guide walks through exactly how to **download a Tumblr video on mobile**, with separate steps for iPhone and Android since the two handle files a little differently.

## The one method that works on every phone

Forget shady apps that ask for your login or bury you in ads. The cleanest approach on any phone is a browser-based tool. You copy the post link, paste it, and save the file straight to your device.

The starting steps are the same everywhere:

1. **Open the Tumblr post** with the video, either in the app or in your mobile browser.
2. **Tap the share icon** on the post (the paper airplane or arrow), then choose **Copy link**.
3. **Open the [Tumblr video downloader](/tumblr-video-downloader)** in your browser.
4. **Paste the link** into the box and tap **Download**.

From here, the saving step depends on your phone. Here's how each one works.

## Saving a Tumblr video on iPhone

iOS is a bit protective about downloads, so you'll route the file through the Files app or Photos.

- After you tap **Download**, Safari shows a download indicator or opens the video.
- If the video plays, **long-press it** and choose **Save to Photos**. The clip lands in your camera roll.
- If iOS offers a download prompt instead, tap **Download**, then open the **Files** app, go to **Downloads**, and find your video there.
- To move it into your camera roll, open the file, tap the share icon, and choose **Save Video**.

That's it. Once it's in Photos, you can edit, trim, or share it like any other clip.

A quick note for older iPhones or iOS versions: if **Save to Photos** doesn't appear, always fall back to **Save to Files**. Every current iPhone can save downloads to Files, and you can copy the video into Photos afterward.

## Saving a Tumblr video on Android

Android is more relaxed about downloads, which makes this even faster.

- After you tap **Download**, Chrome (or your browser) saves the file automatically.
- You'll usually see a **download complete** notification. Tap it to open the video.
- The file is stored in your **Downloads** folder and typically shows up in your **Gallery** or **Photos** app within a minute.
- If it doesn't appear in Gallery right away, open the **Files** app, go to **Downloads**, and you'll find it there.

Some Android browsers ask where to save the file before downloading. Just pick **Downloads** and you'll always know where to look.

## Tips for a smooth mobile save

A few small things prevent the most common headaches:

- **Copy the post permalink, not the blog link.** Tap the post's timestamp to open it on its own page first, then copy that URL. Grabbing the blog's home link is the number one reason downloads fail.
- **Use Wi-Fi for longer clips** so you're not waiting on mobile data or hitting a slow connection.
- **Don't screen record.** It captures your status bar and taps, drops frames, and eats far more storage for a worse result.
- **Check your storage** before saving several videos in a row, especially high-resolution ones.

## What about the Tumblr app's built-in options?

The Tumblr app lets you like, reblog, and share a post, but there's no native download for video. The share menu only sends a link. That's why the copy-link-and-paste method is the go-to. It's also why you don't need to install anything extra or hand over your account details to a third-party app.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where do saved Tumblr videos go on my phone?**

On iPhone, they go to the Files app under Downloads, or to Photos if you choose Save to Photos. On Android, they land in your Downloads folder and usually appear in your Gallery or Photos app shortly after.

**Do I need to install an app to save Tumblr videos?**

No. A browser-based downloader works entirely in Safari or Chrome, so there's nothing to install and no account to connect. That keeps your phone cleaner and your login safe.

**Why does the video save but won't play?**

This almost always means the link pointed to the wrong media, like a photoset or the blog home page instead of the video post. Reopen the specific post by tapping its timestamp, copy that permalink, and try again.
