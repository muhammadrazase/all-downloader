A computer is the best place to download Instagram content. You get full-HD files, a real keyboard for pasting links fast, and a proper Downloads folder that never hides your files. This guide shows you how to **download Instagram videos on PC** and Mac — Reels, feed videos, and IGTV-style clips — using nothing but your browser.

Works on Windows 10 and 11, macOS Ventura through Sequoia, and every major browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.

## The three-step method

No software to install. Just your browser and the link.

1. Open **instagram.com** and find the video or Reel. Log in if you need to.
2. Click the **paper-plane (Share)** icon, or the **three-dot menu** on a feed post, and choose **Copy Link**.
3. Open a new tab, go to our [Instagram video downloader](/instagram-video-downloader), paste the link (**Ctrl+V** on Windows, **Cmd+V** on Mac), and click **Download**.

Pick your resolution — HD is selected by default — and the file saves to your computer.

## Where the file lands

By default, browsers drop downloads in one place:

- **Windows:** `C:\Users\YourName\Downloads` — or press **Ctrl+J** in Chrome/Edge to open the download list and click the file.
- **Mac:** the **Downloads** folder in Finder, or click the download icon in Safari's toolbar (top right) to see it.

Want files somewhere specific? In Chrome or Edge, go to **Settings → Downloads** and turn on **Ask where to save each file**. Now every Instagram video prompts you for a folder — handy if you're organizing clips for a project.

## Copying the link from a browser vs the app

On desktop the Share options differ slightly by post type:

- **Reels:** click the paper-plane icon under the Reel, then **Copy Link**.
- **Feed videos:** click the **three dots** at the top-right of the post, then **Copy Link**.
- **Someone's profile grid:** open the specific post first — copying from the grid page grabs the wrong URL.

If you paste a link and nothing loads, you almost certainly copied the profile URL instead of the post URL. Open the individual video and copy again.

## Why desktop is the better choice

Downloading on a computer has real advantages over mobile:

- **Higher quality.** Desktop reliably serves the full-resolution file, while mobile browsers sometimes downscale.
- **Faster batch saving.** Open several videos in tabs, copy and paste each link, and let downloads queue up.
- **Easy editing next.** The MP4 is right there in Downloads, ready to drop into Premiere, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, or PowerPoint.

And like on mobile, a browser-based tool needs no account and adds no watermark. Never install a desktop "Instagram downloader" program that wants admin access — the browser method is safer and does the same job.

## Quick troubleshooting

**The download saved as `.html` or won't play.** Your browser mislabeled it. Right-click the file, rename the extension to `.mp4`, and it plays normally. Better yet, use the tool's labeled Download button rather than right-click Save.

**Paste does nothing.** You copied a profile or Story link, not a video link. Open the specific Reel or post, then Copy Link again.

**Only public videos work.** Instagram blocks downloads from private accounts. No browser tool can bypass that — you can only save from public profiles.

**Safari opens the video instead of downloading.** Right-click (or Control-click) the video and choose **Download Video**, or use the download button on the tool directly.

**Download is slow or stuck.** Pause and resume from the browser's download list (Ctrl+J / Cmd+Shift+L), or check your connection and retry the link.

## Use downloads responsibly

Saving videos for offline viewing, research, or archiving your own content is perfectly reasonable. Re-uploading another creator's work as your own violates Instagram's terms and can get your account restricted. If you reshare, credit the original creator and confirm they're comfortable with it.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I download an Instagram video on my PC?**

Copy the video's link from instagram.com using the Share icon or three-dot menu, paste it into a browser-based downloader, and click Download. The MP4 saves to your Downloads folder — no software installation and no login required.

**What quality can I download on desktop?**

Desktop browsers reliably serve the highest resolution Instagram provides for that video, typically full HD. Because computers don't downscale the way some mobile browsers do, PC and Mac usually give you the cleanest file available.

**Can I download several Instagram videos at once on my computer?**

Yes. Open each video in its own browser tab, copy and paste the links one after another, and your browser queues the downloads. It's much faster than doing it one at a time on a phone.
