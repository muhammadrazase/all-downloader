# SEO Strategy — Keywords & Backlinks (from competitor research)

Expert playbook based on analysis of the top downloader sites (SnapTik, ssstik, SaveFrom, Y2Mate, tiktokio, tiksave, snapinsta and others). On-page SEO is already built into the site — **this doc is the off-page + content work only you can execute.**

> Reality check: "tiktok downloader" is dominated by sites with millions of backlinks. You win by taking **long-tail keywords first**, then earning links to climb toward the head terms over 6–12 months.

---

## 1. Competitor teardown — what the leaders do

| Competitor | Their play | What to copy |
|---|---|---|
| **SnapTik / ssstik / tiktokio** | Hyper-focused on "TikTok no watermark", 5-second promise, multi-language | Speed + "no watermark" messaging (done), add language versions later |
| **SaveFrom / Y2Mate** | Broad multi-platform, huge backlink profiles, old domains | Multi-platform (done); you can't match their age — go long-tail |
| **snapinsta / sssinstagram** | One platform, exact-match domain, heavy directory backlinks | Directory backlinks (see §4) |
| **MultiSaveApp / noadsdl** | "No ads" + "all platforms" angle, device-specific pages | Device-specific content (iPhone/Android/PC) — you have this in how-to |

**Their common keyword pattern:** `{platform} downloader` + `no watermark` + `HD` + `MP4/MP3` + `online` + `free` + `{device}`.

## 2. Keyword map (target these)

### 🎯 Head terms (high volume, HARD — long-term goal)
`tiktok downloader` · `instagram video downloader` · `youtube downloader` · `facebook video downloader` · `video downloader` · `youtube to mp4` · `youtube to mp3`
→ Your exact-match tool pages already target these. Don't expect ranking for 6–12 months.

### 🥈 Body terms (medium volume, MEDIUM difficulty — realistic in 3–6 months)
- `tiktok downloader without watermark`, `save tiktok video`, `tiktok mp3 download`
- `instagram reels downloader`, `download instagram video`, `igtv downloader`
- `youtube shorts downloader`, `download youtube video hd`, `youtube 4k downloader`
- `facebook reels downloader`, `download facebook video hd`
- `linkedin video downloader`, `save linkedin video`

### 🥇 Long-tail (lower volume, EASY — your fastest wins, target NOW)
These are questions your 30 blog posts should own. Add more posts for any you're missing:
- `how to download tiktok video without watermark on iphone`
- `how to download instagram reels on pc`
- `how to save youtube video to phone`
- `how to download facebook video on android`
- `is it legal to download tiktok videos`
- `how to convert youtube to mp3 free`
- `download tiktok video no watermark hd`
- `best free video downloader no ads`
- `how to download private... ` (careful — only public)
- `download youtube shorts mp4`

> **Rule:** every blog post = ONE long-tail phrase in the title, H1, first paragraph, and one H2. You already do this — replicate for new posts.

### 📝 Content gaps to fill (new blog posts to add — easy ranking)
Per platform, add device-specific + comparison posts:
- "How to download {platform} videos on iPhone (step by step)"
- "How to download {platform} videos on Android"
- "How to download {platform} videos on PC/Mac"
- "{platform} to MP3: how to save audio"
- "Best free {platform} downloaders compared" (link your tool as #1)

## 4. Backlink playbook (the real work) 🔗

Google ranks competitive terms mostly on **backlinks**. Do this over **6–8 weeks**, ~5–10 links/week (never all at once — unnatural velocity gets penalized).

### Tier 1 — High-authority tool/startup directories (DA 50–90) — do these FIRST
Each gives a permanent, indexed, high-authority backlink:
- **Product Hunt** (producthunt.com) — launch it; huge one-day traffic + lasting link
- **AlternativeTo** (alternativeto.net) — ranks for "X alternative" searches
- **SaaSHub** (saashub.com) — ranks for "best {category} tools"
- **Indie Hackers** (indiehackers.com)
- **BetaList** (betalist.com)
- **Launching Next** (launchingnext.com)
- **Uneed** (uneed.best), **Fazier** (fazier.com), **DevHunt** (devhunt.org), **StartupBase** (startupbase.io)
- **There's An AI For That / Futurepedia** (if you add any AI angle)

### Tier 2 — General directories (DA 40+) — spread over weeks
Submit to 15–20 quality directories (skip spammy 500-link lists). Search "free directory submission sites 2026 DA 40" and pick maintained ones. Write a **unique description each time**.

### Tier 3 — Q&A + community (do-follow context links + traffic)
- **Reddit** — answer questions in r/tiktokhelp, r/Instagram, r/youtube, r/software (be helpful, not spammy)
- **Quora** — answer "how to download {platform} video" questions, link naturally
- **Medium / dev.to / Hashnode** — republish a helpful guide with a link back

### Tier 4 — Outreach (highest value, slowest)
- **Guest posts** on small tech/how-to blogs
- **Broken-link building**: find dead downloader links on other sites, suggest yours
- **HARO / Help-a-reporter** style: answer journalist queries in the tech niche

### Submission cadence (natural velocity)
Week 1: Product Hunt + 3 Tier-1 · Week 2–3: rest of Tier-1 + 5 directories · Week 4–6: directories + Q&A · Ongoing: 1 guest post/month.

## 6. 90-day action plan

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| **1** | Verify Google Search Console + Bing, submit `/sitemap.xml`, Product Hunt launch, 3 Tier-1 directories |
| **2–4** | Finish Tier-1, start Tier-2 directories (5/week), publish 3–5 long-tail blog posts |
| **5–8** | Tier-2 directories, Reddit/Quora answers (2–3/week), 1 guest post |
| **9–12** | Guest posts, monitor Search Console for keywords that show impressions, double-down on those with more content |

**Track everything** in a simple sheet: URL submitted, date, status, live link.

---

## 7. Measure
- **Google Search Console** → Performance: watch impressions → clicks → position. Optimize pages that get impressions but low clicks (improve title/description).
- **Analytics** (Plausible/GA — now built in) → which pages/keywords bring traffic.
- Target: first long-tail rankings in **2–3 months**, meaningful traffic by **month 6**.
