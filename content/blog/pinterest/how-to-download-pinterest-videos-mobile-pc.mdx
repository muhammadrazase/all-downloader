Pinterest is packed with short, useful videos: recipes, workout routines, DIY tutorials, outfit ideas. The problem is that Pinterest doesn't give you a "save to device" button. Tapping the save icon only pins the video back to your own boards, where it still lives inside the app.

If you want the actual file on your phone or computer so you can watch it offline, reshare it, or keep it for reference, you need a different method. This guide walks through the fastest ways to **download Pinterest videos** on every device, with no technical steps and no software to install.

## Why you can't save Pinterest videos directly

Pinterest streams video the same way most social platforms do. The file isn't sitting in a single downloadable location; it's delivered in pieces while you watch. That's why the built-in save button only creates a pin, not a file.

A video downloader solves this by reading the pin's public link, finding the underlying video, and packaging it back into a standard MP4 you can store anywhere. Nothing about your account is touched, and you never need to log in.

## The fastest method: use a web downloader

The simplest approach works identically on phones and computers because it runs in your browser. Our [Pinterest video downloader](/pinterest-video-downloader) is free, needs no signup, and adds no watermark to your file.

Here's the whole process:

1. Open Pinterest and find the video you want.
2. Tap the share icon (or the three dots) and choose **Copy link**.
3. Paste that link into the downloader box.
4. Press Download and pick your quality.
5. Save the MP4 to your device.

That's it. The same five steps cover every platform below, so once you've done it once you'll never need to look it up again.

## Download Pinterest videos on iPhone

iPhones handle Pinterest video downloads cleanly now that Safari saves files straight to the Files app and Photos.

1. In the Pinterest app, open the video and tap the share arrow.
2. Choose **Copy link**.
3. Open Safari and go to the downloader.
4. Paste the link and tap Download.
5. When the file loads, tap the share icon and choose **Save to Files** or **Save Video** to send it to your camera roll.

If you only see an option to open the video rather than save it, long-press the video and select **Download** from the menu.

## Download Pinterest videos on Android

Android gives you even more freedom because downloaded files land in your Downloads folder automatically.

1. Open the pin in the Pinterest app.
2. Tap the three dots and select **Copy link**.
3. Open Chrome and visit the downloader.
4. Paste the link and tap Download.
5. Confirm the save. Find your video in **Files > Downloads** or your Gallery.

Some Samsung and Xiaomi browsers ask permission the first time you save a file. Allow it once and every future download goes through without a prompt.

## Download Pinterest videos on PC and Mac

On a computer the process is arguably the smoothest, since you can grab the link and save the file side by side.

1. Open Pinterest in any browser and click the video.
2. Click the share icon and choose **Copy link**, or copy the URL from the address bar.
3. Paste the link into the downloader.
4. Choose your resolution and click Download.
5. The MP4 saves to your default Downloads folder.

This works the same in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari, on both Windows and macOS. There's nothing to install and nothing to configure.

## Tips for better downloads

**Pick the highest resolution offered.** Pinterest videos are usually short, so file sizes stay small even at full quality. There's rarely a reason to choose a lower option.

**Copy the direct pin link, not a board link.** If you paste a link to an entire board, the downloader can't know which video you mean. Open the specific pin first.

**Check the video is public.** Videos on secret boards or from private accounts can't be pulled, because the downloader only sees what's publicly available.

**Save the file soon after copying.** Pinterest link tokens can expire. If a download fails, go back, copy a fresh link, and try again.
