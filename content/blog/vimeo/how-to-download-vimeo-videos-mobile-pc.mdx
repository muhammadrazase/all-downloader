# How to Download Vimeo Videos on Mobile & PC

Vimeo is where filmmakers, agencies, and creators keep their best work. Sometimes you need a local copy: a client wants the final cut offline, you're archiving your own uploads, or you have a video you paid for and want to watch on a flight. This guide covers the practical ways to **download Vimeo videos** on both mobile and desktop, plus the built-in options many people miss.

One thing up front: only download content you own or have explicit rights to. We'll come back to that, but keep it in mind for everything below.

## The fastest method: a web-based downloader

If you just need the file, a browser tool is the simplest route because it works the same on every device.

1. Open the video in the Vimeo app or a browser and copy its URL (it looks like `vimeo.com/123456789`).
2. Paste the link into the [Vimeo downloader](/vimeo-video-downloader).
3. Pick your quality and format, then tap or click **Download**.

That's it. Nothing to install, and it behaves identically whether you're on an iPhone, an Android phone, or a laptop. This is the method most people want because it skips app permissions and account logins.

## Downloading on iPhone and iPad

iOS locks down where apps can save files, so the flow is slightly different from Android.

- Copy the Vimeo link from the app's **Share** menu.
- Paste it into a web downloader in Safari.
- When the file is ready, choose **Download Linked File** or use the share sheet to send it to the **Files** app.
- From Files, you can move the video into Photos or your cloud storage.

If the video is one you uploaded, the Vimeo app itself may show an offline option depending on your plan. Check the three-dot menu on the video before reaching for anything else.

## Downloading on Android

Android is more flexible with storage, so this is usually the smoothest experience.

- Tap **Share**, then **Copy link** on the video.
- Open your browser and paste the link into a downloader.
- Select your quality and save. The file lands in your **Downloads** folder by default.
- Use any file manager to move it into a gallery folder if you want it in your camera roll.

## Downloading on Windows and Mac

Desktop gives you the most control over quality and file naming.

- Copy the video URL from your browser's address bar.
- Paste it into the downloader and choose a resolution. On desktop you'll often see the full range up to 1080p or higher, depending on the source.
- Save the MP4 to a folder you'll remember.

Power users sometimes reach for command-line tools, but for a single video a web tool is faster and far less fiddly. If you want higher resolutions or an audio-only file, see our guide on how to [download Vimeo videos in HD and MP3](/blog/vimeo/download-vimeo-videos-hd-mp3).

## Using Vimeo's own download feature

Before using any third-party tool, check whether the creator already allows downloads. Vimeo lets uploaders enable a native **Download** button under the video. When it's turned on, you'll see a download link right on the page with a menu of resolutions. This is the cleanest, most legitimate option, and it's always worth a look first, especially for your own uploads or videos shared with you by a client.

Vimeo Pro and business accounts can also download their originals directly from the video manager in full source quality.

## Quick tips for clean downloads

- **Match quality to purpose.** A social clip doesn't need a 4K master; a client deliverable does.
- **Check the file after saving.** Play the first few seconds to confirm audio and video are intact.
- **Mind your storage.** High-resolution Vimeo files are large. Clear space before downloading long videos.
- **Keep the link handy.** If a download fails, a fresh copy of the URL often fixes it.

## A note on rights

The most important rule isn't technical. **Only download content you own or have permission to save.** That covers your own uploads, videos a client sent you to review, and clips explicitly licensed to you. Downloading someone else's work to re-post or sell it isn't just against Vimeo's terms, it can be copyright infringement. When in doubt, ask the creator. ## Frequently asked questions

**Can I download a Vimeo video without an account?**
Yes. A web-based downloader doesn't require you to sign in to Vimeo or create an account anywhere. You only need the video's URL and the right to save it.

**Why is the download button missing on a Vimeo video?**
The uploader controls that button. If it's hidden, the creator has disabled native downloads. You can still save videos you have rights to using a downloader, but respect the creator's choice for content that isn't yours.

**What format do Vimeo downloads use?**
Most tools save Vimeo videos as MP4, which plays on virtually every phone, computer, and TV. If you only want the audio, you can convert to MP3 during the download.
