LinkedIn doesn't offer a download button on videos, and Android's gallery won't capture a clip straight from the feed. The good news: on Android you have Chrome and a real file system, which makes saving a native LinkedIn video quick once you know the steps.

This works for native LinkedIn videos — clips uploaded directly to the platform. Reshared YouTube or Vimeo links are hosted elsewhere and need to be downloaded from their original source.

## What you'll need

- The **LinkedIn app** (or LinkedIn in Chrome) to grab the post link.
- **Chrome** or any modern Android browser.
- A minute of your time. No extra app, no account.

## Step 1: Copy the post link

Open the **LinkedIn app** and scroll to the video you want.

1. Tap the **three dots (•••)** at the top-right of the post.
2. Tap **Copy link to post**.

The URL is now on your clipboard. Browsing in Chrome instead? Tap the post's timestamp to open it on its own page, then long-press the address bar and copy the URL.

## Step 2: Paste the link into the downloader

Open **Chrome** and head to our [LinkedIn video downloader](/linkedin-video-downloader).

1. Tap the input field, then **long-press and choose Paste**.
2. Tap **Download**.
3. Give it a moment to fetch the video, then choose your quality — pick **HD (1080p)** if it's available.

The tool grabs the direct MP4 file, so you keep the real video rather than a low-quality screen capture.

## Step 3: Save it to your Downloads folder

When the download link shows up, tap it.

1. Chrome asks you to confirm — tap **Download**.
2. A notification appears at the top of the screen showing progress.
3. When it's done, tap the notification to open the video, or find it later in your **Files / My Files** app under the **Downloads** folder.

Most Android phones also surface downloaded videos automatically in the **Gallery** or **Google Photos** app within a minute, so you may see it there without moving anything.

## Step 4 (optional): Confirm it's in your gallery

If the video doesn't appear in Gallery right away:

1. Open the **Files** (or **My Files** on Samsung) app.
2. Go to **Downloads**.
3. Tap the MP4 to play it, or use the menu to **Move** or **Copy** it into a folder your Gallery scans, such as **DCIM** or **Movies**.

After that, Google Photos and your default gallery will index it.

## Troubleshooting a stuck download

If nothing downloads, run through these:

- **Wrong link.** Use *Copy link to post* — not a link to the author's profile or a specific comment.
- **Not a native video.** An embedded YouTube or Vimeo player isn't LinkedIn-hosted. Download it from that platform instead.
- **Storage permission.** If Chrome asks for permission to save files, allow it. Go to **Settings > Apps > Chrome > Permissions** and enable **Files and media** if you denied it earlier.
- **Private post.** Videos in private groups or behind a login can't be fetched.

## Understanding video quality

You'll only ever get the resolution the uploader originally chose — typically 720p or 1080p for native LinkedIn video. No downloader can invent detail beyond the source file, so grab HD when it's offered and accept the standard version otherwise.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can't I save a LinkedIn video straight from the Android app?

LinkedIn has no built-in download button, and the in-app player blocks long-press saving. Copying the post link and running it through a browser-based downloader is the reliable way to get the file onto your phone.

### Where do LinkedIn video downloads go on Android?

They save to your **Downloads** folder, viewable in the Files or My Files app. Most phones also add the video to Gallery or Google Photos automatically; if not, you can move it into the DCIM or Movies folder so your gallery picks it up.

### Do I need to install an app to download LinkedIn videos on Android?

No. Everything runs in Chrome. You copy the post link, paste it into the online downloader, and save the MP4 to your Downloads folder — no app install and no account required.
