## Download Facebook videos on PC and Mac in HD

A desktop is the best place to **download Facebook videos in HD**. You get a big screen to check the quality, faster wired internet, and a real file system to organize your clips. Best of all, you don't need to install any software, whether you're on Windows or a Mac, a browser is all it takes.

Here's the full process using Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox, plus how to grab the sharpest copy available.

## Step 1: Copy the Facebook video URL

On a computer the link is easy to grab.

- **Right-click the video** while it's playing and choose **Copy video URL** or **Copy link**. (Note: Facebook sometimes shows "Show video URL", copy the address it displays.)
- **Or use the post menu:** Click the three dots (**...**) at the top-right of the post, then **Copy link**.
- **Or copy from the address bar:** Click the video's timestamp to open it on its own page, then copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

Only public videos work. If the post is private or in a closed group, the link won't be downloadable, that's a Facebook privacy rule.

## Step 2: Paste and download

1. Copy the video URL using a method above.
2. Open a new tab and go to our [Facebook video downloader](/facebook-video-downloader).
3. Click the input box and paste the URL (**Ctrl + V** on Windows, **Cmd + V** on Mac).
4. Click **Download**. The available quality options appear.
5. Choose **HD (720p or 1080p)** for the best result, or **SD** for a smaller file.
6. The download starts automatically, or the video opens in a new tab. If it opens, **right-click the video** and choose **Save video as...**

The file saves as an MP4, ready to play in any media player.

## Step 3: Find the file on Windows or Mac

- **Windows:** Open **File Explorer**, then go to **Downloads** (or check the download bar at the bottom of your browser).
- **Mac:** Open **Finder**, click **Downloads** in the sidebar, or click the download icon in Safari's toolbar.

Want it somewhere specific? Right-click the finished download in your browser and choose **Show in folder** (Windows) or **Show in Finder** (Mac), then drag it wherever you like.

## HD vs SD: get the sharpest copy

- **HD (720p / 1080p):** Ideal for large screens, editing, presentations, or archiving. Larger file size.
- **SD (360p / 480p):** Smaller and quicker, fine for casual viewing or slow connections.

A video can only be downloaded in the quality it was originally uploaded. If HD isn't listed, the uploader didn't post it in HD, and no tool can add detail that was never there. On desktop you can preview the clip before saving to confirm the resolution looks right.

## Browser-specific notes

- **Chrome / Edge:** Downloads appear in a bar at the bottom or in the download tray (top-right). Click the file to open it.
- **Safari:** Downloads land in the toolbar's download list (top-right); double-click to open.
- **Firefox:** The download arrow in the toolbar shows recent files; click to open the folder.

If your browser blocks a media download, look for a small prompt and click **Keep** or **Allow**.

## Troubleshooting on desktop

- **Paste does nothing:** The URL wasn't copied. Go back and copy the link again.
- **Video plays instead of downloading:** Right-click it and choose **Save video as...**
- **Only SD is available:** The original was uploaded in standard definition.
- **Link rejected:** The video is likely private, removed, or restricted. Only public videos can be saved.

## Use your downloads responsibly

Saving a public video for personal, offline use is generally acceptable. Re-uploading someone else's video as your own or using it commercially can breach copyright and Facebook's terms of service. Credit creators and get permission before sharing widely, especially for business use.
