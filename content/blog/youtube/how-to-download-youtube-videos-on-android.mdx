## Download YouTube videos on Android without the hassle

Android gives you far more freedom than iPhone when it comes to files — but the YouTube app still won't hand you a real video file. Premium downloads stay trapped inside the app and disappear if you cancel. If you want an actual MP4 or MP3 saved to your phone, you'll go around the app instead.

The best part: you can do it entirely in **Chrome**, and the file lands in your normal **Downloads** folder like any other file. Here's how to **download YouTube videos on Android** in HD or as audio.

## What you'll need

- Any Android phone or tablet (Android 8 or newer)
- Chrome, Samsung Internet, or Firefox
- Nothing else — no APK sideloading, no root

## How to download a YouTube video on Android (HD)

1. Open the **YouTube app** and find your video, or open **youtube.com** in Chrome.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Copy link**. In Chrome, just copy the URL from the address bar.
3. Open a new Chrome tab and go to our [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
4. Paste the link and tap **Download**.
5. Pick your quality — **720p, 1080p Full HD, or 4K** where the video supports it.
6. Tap the download option. Chrome will ask you to confirm; tap **Download** again.

The file saves straight to your **Downloads** folder. Open it from the notification shade, from Chrome's Downloads menu, or through your **Files** / **My Files** app.

**Tip:** If the video opens and plays instead of downloading, tap the three-dot menu on the video and choose **Download**, or long-press and select **Save video**.

## How to save YouTube as MP3 on Android

Only want the audio? Grab an MP3 in a few taps.

1. Copy the YouTube link like above.
2. Paste it into the [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
3. Choose **MP3** or **Audio only**.
4. Tap **Download** and confirm.

The MP3 saves to your Downloads folder. From there you can add it to a music app, set it as a ringtone in Settings, or move it to an SD card.

## Where downloaded videos go on Android

Everything you download through Chrome lands in the **Downloads** folder by default. To find it:

- Open the **Files** (or **My Files** on Samsung) app
- Tap **Downloads**
- Or open Chrome, tap the **three-dot menu**, and choose **Downloads**

Want the video in your Gallery? Most Android phones show downloaded MP4s in the Gallery automatically. If not, move the file into your **DCIM** or **Movies** folder and the Gallery will pick it up.

## Move downloads to an SD card

One advantage Android has: expandable storage. If your phone has an SD card, open the **Files** app, long-press the downloaded video, tap **Move**, and choose your SD card. Great for building an offline library without filling up internal storage.

## Common problems and quick fixes

**Chrome blocks the download.** Tap the notification and choose **Download anyway**, or check Chrome's download settings. Standard MP4 and MP3 files are safe.

**The file won't play.** Android plays MP4 out of the box. If a clip won't open, re-download it as standard 1080p instead of a niche format, or install a free player like VLC.

**Download is slow or stops.** Switch to Wi-Fi for large HD and 4K files. If it stalls, tap the download again to resume or restart it.

**Only low resolutions show up.** The downloader can only offer qualities the creator actually uploaded. If there's no 1080p option, the source video doesn't have one.

## Use downloads responsibly

Download for personal offline use — commutes, flights, patchy signal, or saving your own uploads. Don't redistribute other people's videos or use them commercially without permission. Keep it fair to creators.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I download YouTube videos on Android for free?

Copy the video link from the YouTube app or Chrome, paste it into an online YouTube downloader, choose your quality or MP3, and tap Download. The file saves to your Downloads folder for free, with no app to install.

### Where are downloaded YouTube videos saved on Android?

By default they go to the Downloads folder. Open your Files or My Files app and tap Downloads, or check Chrome's Downloads menu. Downloaded MP4 videos usually appear in the Gallery app too.

### Can I download YouTube videos as MP3 on Android?

Yes. Paste the video link into the downloader and select MP3 or Audio only. The audio file saves to Downloads, ready to add to a music app or set as a ringtone.
