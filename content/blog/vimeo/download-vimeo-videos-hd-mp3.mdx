# How to Download Vimeo Videos in HD (and MP3)

Vimeo built its reputation on quality. Creators upload crisp, high-bitrate video, so it makes sense to want a **Vimeo video download in HD** rather than a blurry, compressed copy. And sometimes you don't need the picture at all, just the audio, whether that's a podcast episode, an interview, or a soundtrack you licensed. This guide shows how to get both.

As always, save only what you own or have permission to download. HD or not, the rights rule doesn't change.

## What "HD" actually means on Vimeo

Vimeo serves the same video in several resolutions so it can adapt to your connection:

- **720p** — standard HD, fine for phones and quick reference.
- **1080p** — full HD, the sweet spot for most deliverables and large screens.
- **2K / 4K** — available when the original was uploaded at that resolution, usually on Pro and business accounts.

The catch: a downloader can only give you a resolution that exists on the source. If the creator uploaded in 1080p, you can't magically pull a 4K version. Always pick the highest option the video actually offers.

## Download Vimeo in HD on desktop

Desktop is the best place for high-resolution files because you get the full quality menu and plenty of storage.

1. Copy the video URL from your browser (`vimeo.com/123456789`).
2. Paste it into the [Vimeo downloader](/vimeo-video-downloader).
3. Look at the quality list and choose **1080p** (or the highest shown).
4. Click **Download** and save the MP4.

Once it's saved, play the first few seconds. A true HD file looks sharp on a full screen; if it looks soft, you may have grabbed a lower resolution, so try again and select the top option.

## Download Vimeo in HD on mobile

The steps mirror desktop, with one thing to watch: file size.

- Tap **Share** on the video and copy the link.
- Paste it into the downloader in your browser.
- Select **720p** or **1080p**. On a phone screen, 720p is often indistinguishable from 1080p and saves storage.
- Save the file, then move it to Photos or Files if you want it in your gallery.

HD videos are large, and cellular data adds up fast. Download over Wi-Fi whenever you can. If you're grabbing several videos or want the whole workflow, our guide on how to [download Vimeo videos on mobile and PC](/blog/vimeo/how-to-download-vimeo-videos-mobile-pc) walks through each device.

## Getting the audio: Vimeo to MP3

Plenty of Vimeo content is worth keeping for the sound alone: recorded talks, music sessions, voiceover work. Converting to MP3 strips out the video and leaves a small, portable audio file.

1. Copy the video URL.
2. Paste it into the downloader.
3. Choose **MP3** (or an audio-only format) instead of a video resolution.
4. Download the file.

A few pointers for good audio:

- **Bitrate matters.** For music, aim for 256 kbps or higher if the tool offers it. For speech, 128 kbps is plenty.
- **Name the file clearly.** Audio files are easy to lose in a Downloads folder.
- **Check the result.** Give it a quick listen to confirm the full track came through.

## Quality tips worth remembering

- **You can't add quality that isn't there.** Downloading a 480p source at "1080p" won't sharpen it. Start with the best available.
- **MP4 + H.264 is the safe bet** for video that plays everywhere. Most Vimeo downloads already use it.
- **Keep the master separate.** If it's your own footage, download your original source file from Vimeo's video manager rather than a re-encoded copy.
- **Storage first.** A few minutes of 1080p can run into hundreds of megabytes. Clear space before you start.

## The rights reminder

High quality doesn't grant new permissions. **Only download content you own or have rights to** — your uploads, licensed music, or videos a creator has cleared for you. Pulling a filmmaker's HD master to reuse without permission is copyright infringement, plain and simple. ## Frequently asked questions

**Can I always download Vimeo in 4K?**
Only if the creator uploaded a 4K source and it's served at that resolution. If the original is 1080p, that's the ceiling. Pick the highest resolution the video actually offers.

**Does converting Vimeo to MP3 reduce audio quality?**
Any conversion involves some compression, but at 256 kbps or higher the difference is inaudible to most listeners. For spoken word, even 128 kbps sounds clean.

**Why did my HD download come out blurry?**
You likely selected a lower resolution, or the source itself is low quality. Copy a fresh URL, choose the top resolution in the list, and confirm the original was uploaded in HD.
