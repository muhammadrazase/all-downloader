## How to download TikTok videos on Android

Android gives you far more freedom than iPhone when it comes to saving files, and downloading TikTok videos is refreshingly simple because of it. Chrome handles downloads cleanly, files land in a predictable **Downloads** folder, and your Gallery picks them up automatically.

This guide covers the fast no-watermark method, where files actually go on Android, and the quirks you will hit on Samsung, Pixel, and other phones.

## Method 1: Download without a watermark using Chrome (recommended)

TikTok's built-in save button stamps a moving watermark and the creator's handle on every clip. To get a clean copy, use a browser-based downloader instead. No app install required.

1. Open **TikTok** and go to the video you want.
2. Tap the **Share arrow** (curved arrow on the right).
3. Tap **Copy link**.
4. Open **Chrome** and visit a [TikTok video downloader](/tiktok-downloader).
5. Paste the link into the box and tap **Download**.
6. Choose the **No watermark** / HD option.

When the download page loads, **tap and hold** the video and choose **Download video**, or tap the download button on the page. Chrome shows a download notification when it finishes.

## Method 2: Find your video in the Downloads folder

Unlike iPhone, Android puts downloaded files somewhere you can actually reach. Here is how to find them.

- **Notification shade:** Swipe down right after the download and tap the completed notification to open the video instantly.
- **Files / My Files app:** Open **Files by Google** or **My Files** (Samsung), then go to **Downloads**. Your video is there.
- **Chrome downloads list:** Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome, then **Downloads**, to see every file you have saved.

On most phones the file also appears in your **Gallery** or **Google Photos** automatically within a minute, since Android scans the Downloads folder for media.

## Method 3: Save directly in the TikTok app (watermark included)

If you are fine with the watermark and the creator allows it, TikTok can save the video straight to your Gallery.

1. Tap the **Share arrow** on the video.
2. Tap **Save video**.
3. The clip goes to your Gallery, usually in a **TikTok** album.

This is the quickest option, but the watermark makes clips look reposted and can lower perceived quality. Use the browser method if you want a clean file.

## Samsung, Pixel, and Xiaomi quirks

Android is not identical across brands, so a couple of things vary:

- **Samsung (One UI):** Downloads open in the **My Files** app, and saved TikToks appear in **Gallery** under a dedicated album. Secure Folder users should note that files saved there will not show in the normal Gallery.
- **Google Pixel:** Everything routes through **Files by Google** and **Google Photos**. If a video does not appear in Photos, open Files and tap the clip to trigger a media scan.
- **Xiaomi / MIUI:** The default browser sometimes blocks background downloads. Switch to **Chrome** if a file will not save.

## Which method is right for you?

- **Want a clean, no-watermark video?** Use Chrome and a web downloader (Method 1).
- **Just saving to rewatch and fine with a watermark?** Use TikTok's save button.
- **Cannot find your file?** Check the **Downloads** folder first, then your **Gallery**.

## Keep quality high and stay fair

A downloader can only save the resolution TikTok actually serves, so a low-quality upload stays low-quality no matter which tool you use. Choose the **HD** option when it is offered.

And a reminder: saving videos for personal viewing is fine, but reposting someone else's work as your own is not. Credit creators and respect content you do not have rights to.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do downloaded TikTok videos go on Android?

Files saved through Chrome go to your **Downloads** folder, which you can open in **Files by Google** or **My Files**. Android usually copies media into your **Gallery** or **Google Photos** automatically within a minute, so check there too.

### How do I download a TikTok without the watermark on Android?

Copy the video's share link from the TikTok app, open **Chrome**, and paste it into a browser-based downloader that offers a **no watermark** option. Choose the HD, watermark-free file and it saves straight to your Downloads folder.

### Do I need an app to download TikTok videos?

No. A browser like Chrome plus a web-based downloader is enough, and it avoids the ads and permissions that many downloader apps ask for. Standalone apps can be convenient for bulk saving, but they are not required.
