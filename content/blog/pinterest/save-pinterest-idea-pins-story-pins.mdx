Idea pins (Pinterest's multi-page format, sometimes still called story pins) are different from a regular video pin. Instead of one clip, they're a stack of pages: video, photos, text overlays, music, and interactive stickers, all swiped through like a story.

That structure is exactly why saving them is confusing. When people try to **download Pinterest idea pins** the normal way, they either get nothing or grab just the first frame. This guide explains what's actually inside an idea pin and how to save the parts you want on mobile and desktop.

## Idea pins vs. regular video pins

A standard video pin is a single video file, so a downloader can pull it in one step. An idea pin is a container. Each page can be a separate video clip or a still image, and Pinterest displays them in sequence.

Because of that, "downloading an idea pin" usually means one of two things:

- Saving one specific page or clip you liked.
- Saving several pages and keeping them as separate files.

Knowing which you want makes the whole process simpler. There's no single file that represents the entire idea pin, so you work page by page.

## Save an idea pin video on mobile

The reliable method is to copy the pin link and let a downloader separate out the video pages for you.

1. Open the idea pin in the Pinterest app.
2. Swipe to the page you want to save (the downloader reads the whole pin, but knowing your target helps).
3. Tap the three dots and choose **Copy link**.
4. Open your browser and go to our [Pinterest video downloader](/pinterest-video-downloader).
5. Paste the link and tap Download.
6. Choose the clip you want and save the MP4 to your device.

On iPhone the file saves through the share sheet into Photos or Files. On Android it drops straight into your Downloads folder.

## Save an idea pin on PC or Mac

Desktop makes it easier to handle multi-page pins because you can see everything at once.

1. Open Pinterest in your browser and click into the idea pin.
2. Copy the link from the share menu or the address bar.
3. Paste it into the downloader.
4. Pick the page or clip you need and click Download.
5. Repeat for any other pages you want. Each one saves as its own file.

This is the cleanest way to archive a full tutorial idea pin, since you end up with each step as a separate video you can reorder or edit later.

## Saving a still image from an idea pin

If the page you want is a photo rather than a video, you don't need a video tool at all.

- **On desktop:** right-click the image and choose **Save image as**.
- **On mobile:** press and hold the image and tap **Download** or **Save image**.

Keep in mind that any text stickers, polls, or music the creator added are part of Pinterest's interface, not the image file. Those interactive elements won't come through in a saved photo or video.

## What you can't save

Idea pins were built to keep people inside Pinterest, so a few things are off limits by design:

- **Interactive stickers and polls** don't export; they only work inside the app.
- **Licensed background music** may be stripped or muted depending on the pin.
- **Private or unpublished idea pins** can't be reached, because the tool only sees public content.

If a download comes back silent or missing a page, one of these is usually the reason.

## Practical tips

**Copy the link, don't screen-record.** Screen recording captures your whole display, including the swipe animation and interface buttons. Copying the link and downloading gives you a clean file.

**Work one page at a time.** Treating each page as its own download avoids confusion and gives you the highest quality for every clip.

**Grab it while it's live.** Creators can delete idea pins at any time. If it's something you rely on, like a recipe or a repair walkthrough, save it sooner rather than later.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I download an entire idea pin as one file?**
No. Idea pins are made of separate pages, so there's no single combined file. You save each video clip or image individually, then keep or edit them as you like.

**Why is there no sound on my saved idea pin clip?**
Many idea pins use licensed music that Pinterest handles separately from the video. When the clip is saved, that background track is often removed, leaving the original recorded audio or none at all.

**Is saving an idea pin different from saving a normal video?**
Slightly. The steps are the same (copy link, paste, download), but because an idea pin holds multiple pages, you'll choose which page to save rather than getting one automatic file.
