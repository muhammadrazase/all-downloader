A VOD, short for Video On Demand, is the full recording of a past Twitch broadcast. Unlike clips, which are tiny highlights, a VOD is the entire stream, sometimes six or eight hours of gameplay, chat, and everything in between. The catch is that Twitch does not keep VODs forever, and it does not make them easy to save.

If you stream, or if you just want to keep a broadcast before it disappears, here is how to **download a Twitch VOD** the right way.

## VODs expire, so timing matters

This is the single most important thing to understand. Twitch automatically deletes VODs after a set window, and the exact length depends on the account:

- Standard channels keep VODs for **7 days**.
- Affiliates, Partners, Turbo, and Prime users keep them for **60 days**.

After that window, the VOD is gone unless it was saved as a Highlight or exported. That means if you want a broadcast, you should download it well before the deadline, not on the last day when you might forget.

Streamers should treat this as a routine habit. Your best content, funniest moments, and clip-worthy plays all live in VODs, and once they expire you cannot get them back.

## The easiest method: paste the VOD link

The fastest approach uses your VOD's URL and a downloader tool. It works from any browser, so the steps are nearly identical on desktop and mobile.

1. On Twitch, go to your channel and open **Video Producer** (Creator Dashboard) or find the VOD under a channel's **Videos** tab.
2. Open the VOD and copy the URL from the address bar. It looks like `twitch.tv/videos/1234567890`.
3. Paste that link into a downloader and choose your quality.
4. Download the MP4 to your device.

Because VODs are long, expect the file to be large and the processing to take longer than a short clip. A multi-hour broadcast in 1080p can easily run several gigabytes, so make sure you have the storage before you start.

## Downloading your own VODs from the dashboard

If it is your channel, Twitch actually gives you a native export option, and it is worth knowing about.

- Open your **Creator Dashboard**, then go to **Content > Video Producer**.
- Find the broadcast you want and click the three-dot menu.
- Choose **Download**.

This exports the full-quality source file straight from Twitch. It is the cleanest option for your own content because you get the original recording with no re-compression. The downside is that it only works for VODs you own and only while they are still within the retention window.

## Downloading on PC vs mobile

**On PC**, downloading a VOD is straightforward. You have storage, a real browser, and a Downloads folder that can handle a multi-gigabyte file. This is the recommended device for anything long.

**On mobile**, it is possible but less comfortable. Long VODs eat phone storage fast, and a dropped connection can interrupt a large download. If you must use a phone, download over Wi-Fi, keep the screen awake, and consider grabbing a lower resolution to keep the file size manageable.

For most people, the honest answer is simple: download VODs on a computer and only use your phone for short clips.

## Choose the right quality

VOD downloaders usually let you pick a resolution. The rule is the same as with clips, you cannot exceed the quality the stream was broadcast in. If you streamed in 1080p60, you can download 1080p60. If your upload was capped at 720p, that is your ceiling.

Pick the highest quality if you plan to edit or archive. Choose a lower resolution only when you are tight on space or bandwidth, since a full broadcast at max quality can be enormous.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long do I have before my VOD is deleted?

Standard accounts keep VODs for 7 days. Affiliates, Partners, Prime, and Turbo users get 60 days. After that the VOD is permanently removed unless you saved it as a Highlight or downloaded it.

### Can I download someone else's VOD?

Technically you can if the VOD is public and still available, but the content belongs to that streamer. Downloading for personal viewing is one thing, reposting their broadcast publicly without permission is another. Always credit and respect the creator's rights.

### Why is my VOD download so large?

VODs are full-length recordings, often several hours. A long broadcast at 1080p60 can be multiple gigabytes. Choose a lower resolution or trim the section you actually need if file size is a problem.
