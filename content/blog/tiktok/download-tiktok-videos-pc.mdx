## How to download TikTok videos on PC and Mac

A computer is the easiest place to download TikTok videos. You get a full browser, a real Downloads folder, right-click controls, and a big screen to check quality before you save. Whether you are on **Windows** or a **Mac**, the process is nearly identical.

This guide covers the clean no-watermark method, the right-click trick, where files land on each system, and how to grab the TikTok link from a desktop browser.

## Step 1: Copy the TikTok video link on desktop

On a computer you usually watch TikTok in the browser rather than an app, so grabbing the link is slightly different.

1. Open **tiktok.com** and play the video you want.
2. Click the **Share** icon (arrow) on the right of the video.
3. Click **Copy link**.

You can also just copy the URL straight from your browser's address bar while the video is open. Either works.

## Step 2: Download without a watermark (recommended)

TikTok does not offer a clean desktop download, and the app's save button adds a watermark. A browser-based tool fixes both.

1. Open a new tab and go to a [TikTok video downloader](/tiktok-downloader).
2. **Paste** the link into the box (Ctrl+V on Windows, Cmd+V on Mac).
3. Click **Download**.
4. Choose the **No watermark** / **HD** option.

The video will either save straight away or open on a results page. If it opens, move to the right-click method below.

## Step 3: Right-click to save the video file

Desktop browsers let you save a playing video directly, which is handy when a download button opens the clip instead of saving it.

- **Windows (Chrome, Edge, Firefox):** Right-click the playing video and choose **Save video as**. Pick a folder and click Save.
- **Mac (Safari, Chrome):** Control-click or right-click the video and choose **Download Video** or **Save Video As**.

If right-click is disabled on the page, use the downloader's dedicated **Download** button instead, which links straight to the file.

## Where your downloads go

Knowing the default folder saves a lot of hunting.

- **Windows:** Files land in **This PC > Downloads** by default. Open **File Explorer** and click **Downloads** in the left sidebar.
- **Mac:** Files go to the **Downloads** folder in Finder, and a download icon appears in your **Dock** for quick access.
- **Custom location:** In any browser you can set **Ask where to save each file** under download settings, so you choose the folder every time.

## Get the best quality on a big screen

The advantage of downloading on a computer is that you can confirm quality before saving. A few tips:

- Always pick the **HD** or highest-resolution option the tool offers.
- Remember a downloader cannot upscale a low-quality upload, so a soft original stays soft.
- MP4 is the most compatible format and plays on virtually every editor and player, so keep it unless you have a reason not to.

## Which approach should you use?

- **Want a clean file for editing or reposting with credit?** Use the no-watermark browser method.
- **Video opened instead of downloading?** Right-click and **Save video as**.
- **Downloading in bulk?** Desktop is ideal, since you can paste links quickly and manage files in one folder.

## Stay on the right side of fair use

Saving videos to watch offline or to keep a personal archive is reasonable. Re-uploading someone else's content as your own is not. Credit creators, and do not redistribute footage you do not have permission to use.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I download a TikTok video on my laptop without a watermark?

Copy the video link from tiktok.com, open a browser-based downloader in a new tab, paste the link, and choose the **no watermark** HD option. The clean MP4 file saves to your **Downloads** folder on both Windows and Mac.

### Why does right-clicking a TikTok video not let me save it?

Some pages disable the right-click menu or load the video through a player that blocks direct saving. When that happens, use a dedicated downloader's **Download** button, which links straight to the video file so you can save it normally.

### What format are downloaded TikTok videos, and will they play on my computer?

Most downloads come as **MP4**, which plays on Windows Media Player, QuickTime, VLC, and every common video editor without conversion. If a tool offers other formats, MP4 remains the safest choice for compatibility.
