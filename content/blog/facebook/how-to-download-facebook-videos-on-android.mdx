## Download Facebook videos on Android without extra apps

Android makes this easier than iPhone does, because Chrome can save files straight to your **Downloads** folder. You don't need a sketchy app from an unknown store or one that stamps a watermark on your clip. With the video link and a browser, you can **download Facebook videos on your Android phone** in under a minute.

Below is the exact process, plus where the file ends up and how to pick HD over a low-res copy.

## Step 1: Copy the Facebook video link

Everything starts with the share link.

- **In the Facebook app:** Tap the three dots (**⋮**) at the top-right of the post, then tap **Copy link**.
- **For a Reel:** Tap **Share**, then **Copy link**.
- **In a mobile browser:** Long-press the video or tap the post's time/date, then choose **Copy link**.

If the post is private or in a closed group you can't view publicly, the link won't work. Only public videos can be saved.

## Step 2: Download using Chrome

1. Copy the link using the steps above.
2. Open **Chrome** and visit our [Facebook video downloader](/facebook-video-downloader).
3. Tap the box and **paste** the link (long-press, then **Paste**).
4. Tap **Download**. Quality options will appear.
5. Pick **HD** for the best resolution or **SD** for a smaller file.
6. Chrome shows a **Download** prompt or starts saving automatically. If the video plays in a new tab instead, long-press it and tap **Download video**.

That's it. The file saves as an MP4 to your phone.

## Step 3: Find your downloaded video

- Pull down the **notification shade** right after downloading and tap the download notice to open it.
- Or open the **Files** app (or **My Files** on Samsung) and go to **Downloads**.
- The video also appears in your **Gallery** or **Google Photos** within a minute or two, since Android scans the Downloads folder for media.

If it doesn't show in Gallery immediately, don't worry, it's still in Downloads. Some phones take a moment to index new files.

## HD vs SD on Android: what to choose

- **HD (720p / 1080p):** Best for full-screen viewing, saving to your Gallery, or sharing later. Bigger file.
- **SD (360p / 480p):** Lighter on storage and data, good for quick clips over mobile data.

Keep in mind a video can only be downloaded in the quality it was uploaded. If HD isn't offered, the original just wasn't posted in HD.

## Troubleshooting on Android

- **Paste option missing:** The link wasn't copied. Return to Facebook and tap **Copy link** again.
- **Download blocked:** Chrome sometimes warns about media files. Tap **Download anyway** if the prompt appears.
- **File won't open in Gallery:** Open it from the **Downloads** folder in your Files app instead; it plays fine there.
- **Nothing downloads:** The video may be private, deleted, or age-restricted. Only public content works.

## Samsung, Xiaomi, and other brands

The steps are the same on any Android phone because they rely on Chrome, not a manufacturer feature. On Samsung the file manager is called **My Files**; on Xiaomi it's the **File Manager** app. Look for the **Downloads** folder either way. If you use a different browser like Firefox or Samsung Internet, the flow is identical, just look for that browser's download prompt.

## Use downloaded videos responsibly

Saving a public video for personal, offline viewing is generally acceptable. Reposting someone else's work as your own or using it commercially can violate copyright and Facebook's terms of service. Credit creators and ask permission when you plan to share widely.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where do Facebook videos save on Android?

They save to your **Downloads** folder as an MP4 file. You can open them from the notification shade right after downloading, from the **Files** or **My Files** app under **Downloads**, or from your **Gallery** once the phone finishes indexing the new file.

### Do I need an app to download Facebook videos on Android?

No. Chrome (or any Android browser) can save the file directly, so a web-based downloader is enough. Avoid random apps that demand permissions or add watermarks, they aren't necessary for this.

### Can I download Facebook videos in HD on Android?

Yes, as long as the video was uploaded in HD. Choose the **HD** option after pasting the link. If only SD appears, the uploader posted the clip in standard definition and no tool can upscale it.
