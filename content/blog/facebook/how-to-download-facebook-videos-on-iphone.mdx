## Download Facebook videos on iPhone the simple way

Facebook still doesn't give you a built-in "save to phone" button for most videos. On an iPhone that's more annoying than usual, because iOS keeps downloads locked inside apps. The good news: you don't need to install anything. Safari plus the Files app is all it takes to **download Facebook videos on your iPhone** and keep them offline.

This guide walks through the exact taps, where the file lands, and how to grab HD instead of a blurry SD copy.

## What you need first: the video link

Every method starts with copying the video's link. Here's how to get it inside the Facebook app or browser.

- **From the Facebook app:** Tap the three dots (**...**) in the top-right corner of the post, then tap **Copy link**.
- **From a Reel:** Tap the **share arrow**, then **Copy link**.
- **From Facebook in Safari:** Long-press the video, or use the post's timestamp, then choose **Copy link**.

If the video is on a private profile or a group you're not in, the link won't work anywhere. That's a Facebook privacy rule, not a limitation of any tool.

## Step-by-step: save to your iPhone with Safari

1. Copy the video link using the steps above.
2. Open **Safari** and go to our [Facebook video downloader](/facebook-video-downloader).
3. Tap the input box and **paste** the link (tap and hold, then **Paste**).
4. Press **Download**. You'll see quality options appear.
5. Choose **HD** for the best quality or **SD** if you want a smaller file.
6. The video opens in a new tab. Tap the **share icon** (the square with an arrow), then tap **Save to Files** or **Save Video**.

If you pick **Save Video**, the clip goes straight to your **Photos / Camera Roll**. If you pick **Save to Files**, choose a folder like **Downloads** on **My iPhone** or **iCloud Drive**.

**Tip:** On iOS, tapping "Save Video" only appears when the tab shows the raw video player. If you only see "Save to Files," that's fine, you can move it to Photos later from the Files app.

## HD vs SD: which should you pick on iPhone?

- **HD (720p or 1080p):** Sharper, better for watching full-screen or sharing later. Larger file size.
- **SD (360p or 480p):** Smaller, quicker to download on slow data, fine for quick clips.

Not every Facebook video was uploaded in HD. If you only see one quality option, that's the highest the uploader posted, no tool can add detail that isn't there.

## Where did my video go?

- **Saved as a photo:** Open the **Photos** app, it appears in **Recents**.
- **Saved to Files:** Open the **Files** app, tap **Browse**, then **Downloads** (or the folder you chose).

To move a video from Files into your Camera Roll, open it in Files, tap the **share icon**, then **Save Video**.

## Common problems and quick fixes

- **"Paste" won't appear:** You didn't copy the link. Go back to Facebook and tap **Copy link** again.
- **Download opens but won't save:** Make sure you're in **Safari**, not the in-app browser. In-app browsers block the save menu on iOS.
- **Video is sideways or won't play:** Re-download and pick the other quality; occasionally one file is corrupt.
- **Nothing happens after pasting:** The video may be private or age-restricted. Only public videos can be downloaded.

## A quick word on doing this responsibly

Downloading a public video to watch offline is generally fine for personal use. Re-uploading someone else's video as your own, or using it commercially, can break copyright and Facebook's terms. When in doubt, credit the creator or ask permission.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I download Facebook videos on iPhone without an app?

Yes. You only need Safari and the Files or Photos app, both built into iOS. Copy the video link, paste it into a web-based downloader, choose HD or SD, and save. There's nothing to install.

### Why can't I save the video to my Camera Roll?

Usually because you're using Facebook's in-app browser instead of Safari, which hides the iOS save menu. Open the downloader in Safari directly, or save to the Files app first and then tap **Save Video** to move it into Photos.

### Can I download private Facebook videos on iPhone?

No. Only videos on public posts, Pages, or public groups can be downloaded. Private profiles and closed groups block the link, and no legitimate tool can bypass those privacy settings.
