## Download YouTube videos on iPhone the easy way

iPhone makes saving videos harder than it should be. There's no download button in the YouTube app unless you pay for Premium, and even then the file stays locked inside the app. If you want a real MP4 or MP3 file you can play offline, move to another device, or edit, you need a different approach.

The good news: you don't need to install a sketchy app or jailbreak anything. Safari and the built-in Files app are all you need. Here's exactly how to **download YouTube videos on iPhone** in HD or as audio.

## What you'll need

- An iPhone running iOS 15 or newer (works on iOS 18 and 26 too)
- The Safari browser (already installed)
- A minute of free time

That's it. No signup, no App Store download.

## How to download a YouTube video on iPhone (HD)

1. Open the **YouTube app** or **youtube.com** in Safari and find the video you want.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Copy Link** (or copy the URL from Safari's address bar).
3. Open a new Safari tab and go to our [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
4. Paste the link into the box and tap **Download**.
5. Choose your quality — **720p, 1080p HD, or 4K** if the original video supports it.
6. When the video opens, tap the **Share** icon, then **Save to Files**.
7. Pick a folder (On My iPhone or iCloud Drive) and tap **Save**.

Your video is now a real MP4 file. Open the **Files app** any time to watch it offline, AirDrop it, or drop it into an editing app.

**Tip:** If the video plays instead of downloading, long-press the video and choose **Download Linked File** or **Save to Files** from the menu.

## How to save a YouTube video as MP3 on iPhone

Want just the audio — a song, podcast, or lecture? The steps are almost identical.

1. Copy the YouTube link the same way as above.
2. Paste it into the [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
3. Select **MP3** or **Audio only** as the format.
4. Tap **Download**, then **Save to Files**.

Now you have an MP3 you can play in the background, add to a playlist app, or use as a ringtone source. This is the cleanest way to pull audio without recording your screen.

## Where do downloaded videos go on iPhone?

By default they land in the **Files app**, in whatever folder you picked during the save step. To find them:

- Open **Files**
- Tap **Browse** at the bottom
- Look under **On My iPhone** or **iCloud Drive**

To move a video into your camera roll, open it in Files, tap **Share**, then **Save Video**. It'll appear in the Photos app so it syncs and plays like any other clip.

## Why not just use a screen recorder?

Screen recording works, but it's a poor substitute. You get:

- Lower quality with visible compression
- Recorded UI elements, notifications, and taps
- No clean audio-only option
- A file that's often the wrong length

A proper downloader gives you the **original resolution** straight from the source — up to 4K — with clean audio and no on-screen clutter.

## Common problems and quick fixes

**The download button does nothing.** Make sure you copied the full link and pasted it without extra spaces. Refresh the page and try again.

**Video saved but won't play.** iPhone plays MP4 natively. If a file won't open, re-download it in a standard quality like 1080p instead of a specialized format.

**I can't find the file.** Check the Files app under both "On My iPhone" and "iCloud Drive" — the save location depends on what you tapped. Use the search bar in Files if you're stuck.

**Only low quality is available.** Some videos are only uploaded in lower resolutions. You can't download a quality the creator never published.

## A quick word on responsible downloading

Save videos for personal, offline use — a trip without Wi-Fi, a class you want to rewatch, or your own content. Don't re-upload someone else's work or use downloads commercially without permission. Respect creators and copyright.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I download YouTube videos on iPhone without an app?

Yes. Using Safari and the built-in Files app, you can save videos and MP3s without installing anything from the App Store. Just copy the link, paste it into an online downloader, and choose Save to Files.

### Where are my downloaded YouTube videos stored on iPhone?

They're saved in the Files app, under either "On My iPhone" or "iCloud Drive," in the folder you selected. To watch a video in your camera roll, open it in Files and tap Share, then Save Video.

### Can I download YouTube videos in 4K on iPhone?

You can download in 4K if the original video was uploaded in 4K. Pick the highest resolution offered in the downloader. Keep in mind 4K files are large, so make sure you have storage space free.
