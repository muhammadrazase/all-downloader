## How to download TikTok videos on iPhone

iPhone makes video downloads feel harder than they should. Apple keeps files locked inside apps, Safari handles downloads differently than Chrome, and TikTok's built-in save button stamps a moving watermark across your clip. This guide fixes all of that.

Below are the real methods that work on iOS today, including how to get a clean, watermark-free file straight into your Photos or Files app.

## Method 1: Save without a watermark using a browser tool (recommended)

TikTok's own **Save video** option adds a watermark and the creator's username. If you want a clean copy, use a web-based downloader instead. This is the fastest route and needs no app install.

1. Open the **TikTok app** and find the video you want.
2. Tap the **Share arrow** on the right side of the screen.
3. Tap **Copy link**.
4. Open **Safari** and go to a [TikTok video downloader](/tiktok-downloader).
5. Paste the link into the box and tap **Download**.
6. Choose the **No watermark** / HD option.

When the video opens, tap and hold it, then choose **Download Video** or the download icon in Safari. On newer iPhones the file lands in your **Files app** under Downloads. From there you can move it to Photos.

**Tip:** If the file plays instead of saving, tap the small download arrow in Safari's address bar (top-right). iOS keeps a download list there.

## Method 2: Move a download from Files into Photos

Videos saved through Safari usually go to the **Files** app first, not your camera roll. Here is how to get them into Photos so they show up in your gallery.

1. Open the **Files** app.
2. Tap **Browse**, then **Downloads**.
3. Find your video and **tap and hold** it.
4. Choose **Share**.
5. Tap **Save Video**.

The clip now appears in **Photos** and is ready to post, text, or edit like any other video.

## Method 3: Use the TikTok save button (watermark included)

If you do not mind the watermark, TikTok can save the video directly, as long as the creator allows downloads.

1. Tap the **Share arrow** on the video.
2. Tap **Save video**.
3. The video goes straight to your camera roll.

This is the simplest option, but the moving watermark makes the clip look reposted and lower quality. Use a browser tool if you plan to reuse the footage.

## Method 4: Build an iOS Shortcut for one-tap downloads

If you save TikToks often, a **Shortcut** turns the whole process into a single tap from the share sheet. You do not need to code anything.

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app (built into iOS).
2. Create a new shortcut that accepts a **URL** as input.
3. Add a **Get Contents of URL** action pointed at a downloader service.
4. Add a **Save to Photo Album** action at the end.
5. Enable **Show in Share Sheet** in the shortcut settings.

Once it is set up, you just tap Share in TikTok, pick your shortcut, and the video saves automatically. Community shortcuts for this exist too, but always review what a downloaded shortcut does before running it, since it can access your links and files.

## Which method should you use?

- **Want a clean, no-watermark file?** Use the browser method (Method 1).
- **Just archiving for yourself and fine with a watermark?** Use TikTok's save button.
- **Download dozens of clips a week?** Set up a Shortcut once and forget it.

## A quick note on quality and etiquette

Web downloaders return the highest resolution TikTok makes available, but they cannot upscale a low-quality upload. If the original clip is soft, the download will be too.

Also worth remembering: downloading for personal viewing is one thing, reposting someone else's video as your own is another. Credit the creator, and do not re-upload content you do not have rights to.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why does my TikTok download have a watermark?

TikTok's own **Save video** button adds the watermark and username automatically. To avoid it, copy the video link and run it through a web-based downloader that offers a **no watermark** option, then save the clean file to your Photos.

### Where do saved TikTok videos go on my iPhone?

It depends on the method. TikTok's built-in save sends videos straight to **Photos** (your camera roll). Files downloaded through Safari usually land in the **Files** app under **Downloads**, so you may need to share them into Photos manually.

### Can I download a TikTok video that has downloads disabled?

Yes, in most cases. Even when a creator turns off in-app downloads, copying the video's share link and pasting it into a browser-based downloader still works, because the link points to the publicly viewable video.
