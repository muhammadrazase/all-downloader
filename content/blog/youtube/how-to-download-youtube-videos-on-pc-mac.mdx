## Download YouTube videos on PC and Mac in full quality

A computer is the best place to download YouTube videos. You have a big screen to pick the exact quality, plenty of storage for large files, and a real file system to organize everything. Whether you're on Windows or macOS, you can pull down **4K video, 1080p HD, or a clean MP3** straight from your browser.

No desktop software to install. No account. Here's exactly how to **download YouTube videos on PC** and Mac.

## What you'll need

- Any Windows PC or Mac
- Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox, or Brave — any modern browser
- An internet connection

That's the whole list.

## How to download a YouTube video on PC or Mac (4K & HD)

1. Open the video on **youtube.com** in your browser.
2. Copy the URL from the **address bar** (or click **Share**, then **Copy**).
3. Open a new tab and go to our [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
4. Paste the link into the box and press **Download**.
5. Choose your quality — **720p, 1080p Full HD, 1440p, or 4K (2160p)** if the video was uploaded that high.
6. Click the download button. The file saves to your **Downloads** folder.

On Windows, find it in **File Explorer under Downloads**. On Mac, open **Finder** and click **Downloads** in the sidebar. From there you can drag the MP4 into any player, editor, or cloud folder.

**Tip:** If the video opens in a new tab and plays, right-click it and choose **Save Video As** to store the file.

## How to download YouTube audio as MP3 on desktop

Perfect for music, podcasts, interviews, or background audio you want on repeat.

1. Copy the YouTube video URL.
2. Paste it into the [YouTube video downloader](/youtube-video-downloader).
3. Select **MP3** or **Audio only** as the format.
4. Click **Download**.

The MP3 saves to your Downloads folder, ready to drop into your music library, a playlist, or an editing project. Pulling audio this way keeps quality high and skips the mess of screen recording.

## Why 4K matters on a computer

Your phone screen hides a lot of detail, but a monitor or TV shows every pixel. If a creator uploaded in 4K, downloading at that resolution gives you:

- Crisp footage for editing or re-framing
- Clean playback on large displays and TVs
- Future-proof files that still look sharp years later

Just remember 4K files are large — a few minutes can run into the gigabytes — so keep an eye on disk space.

## Organize your downloads

On a desktop you can actually build a library. A few habits that help:

- Create folders by topic (Music, Tutorials, Archive)
- Rename files right after download so you can find them later
- Move finished downloads out of the Downloads folder so it doesn't become a dumping ground
- Back up anything important to an external drive or cloud storage

## Common problems and quick fixes

**The download saves as the wrong file type.** Double-check you selected MP4 for video or MP3 for audio before clicking download.

**4K isn't available.** The downloader can only offer resolutions the creator uploaded. If there's no 4K option, the source video tops out lower.

**Playback stutters.** Very high-bitrate 4K can tax older computers. Play it in VLC, or download 1080p for smoother playback on modest hardware.

**Browser warns about the file.** Standard MP4 and MP3 downloads are safe. Click **Keep** if your browser asks, and only download from a source you trust.
